# Code of Conduct

Oxygen Weather contributors are expected to keep project spaces professional, technical, and constructive.

Be respectful of different experience levels and backgrounds. Critique code, data, methods, and decisions rather than people. Do not harass, threaten, demean, or deliberately disrupt contributors. Do not publish another person’s private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive, off-topic, deceptive, or unsafe contributions and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its contributors.

For conduct concerns, use a private maintainer contact channel if one is listed by the repository host. Do not disclose sensitive personal information in a public issue.
